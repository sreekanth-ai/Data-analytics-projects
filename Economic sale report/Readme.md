## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **E-Commerce Sales Dashboard** developed in **Power BI** to analyze sales performance across countries, states, markets, product categories, and shipping modes.

The dashboard helps businesses monitor key KPIs, identify high-performing regions, and gain insights into customer purchasing trends for better decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance
* Identify top-performing countries and states
* Understand category-wise sales distribution
* Evaluate shipping mode performance
* Explore market contribution to total sales
* Build an interactive business intelligence dashboard

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

### 🔹 Top 5 Sales by Country

* United States recorded the highest sales contribution
* Australia and France also showed strong sales performance

### 🔹 Top 5 Sales by State

* England generated the highest sales
* California and Ile-de-France were among the top-performing states

### 🔹 Sales by Category

* Technology category achieved the highest sales
* Furniture and Office Supplies also contributed significantly

### 🔹 Sales by Market

* APAC market had the highest share in total sales
* Followed by EU and US markets

### 🔹 Sales by Ship Mode

* Standard Class shipping mode handled the majority of sales
* Same Day shipping contributed the least

## 🧮 DAX Measures Used

### Total Sales

```DAX id="p5q4u7"
Total Sales = SUM(Orders[Sales])
```

### Total Profit

```DAX id="cz9x1k"
Total Profit = SUM(Orders[Profit])
```

### Total Quantity

```DAX id="r2v7lm"
Total Quantity = SUM(Orders[Quantity])
```

### Shipping Cost

```DAX id="a9w3bx"
Shipping Cost = SUM(Orders[Shipping Cost])
```
